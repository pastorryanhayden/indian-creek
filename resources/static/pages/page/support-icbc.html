Support ICBC

Indian Creek Baptist Camp recently wrapped up a wonderful fall retreat season, hosting a teen group for an autumn weekend in early November. It was refreshing to watch young people slow down, spend extra time in God’s Word, and enjoy meaningful moments together around a campfire—s’mores and all.

As we enter the Thanksgiving season, our hearts are full of gratitude. For nearly three decades, God has used the faithful support of churches, families, businesses, and individuals to strengthen this ministry. Everywhere you look on the camp property, you can see the impact of someone’s generosity—updated facilities, restored buildings, and improvements that help us serve young people with excellence.

From repaired floors and roofs to refreshed dorms, fencing, and decking, Indian Creek Baptist Camp simply would not be what it is today without the kindness and investment of God’s people.

As we approach our 30th anniversary, we are excited to keep moving forward. This November, we plan to focus on much-needed updates to the tabernacle. After many seasons of use, the chairs need to be replaced. We also hope to build a small sound booth and storage closet to better protect our sound and video equipment, as well as extend the back wall of the platform to improve screen visibility during morning services.

We ask that you prayerfully consider giving a monetary gift during this season of thanksgiving and generosity. Your investment directly impacts the next generation of young people who will come to Indian Creek to grow stronger in the Lord. God is still working under the tabernacle at ICBC. We are praying for His continued blessing and inviting you to be a part of what He is doing—changing lives, one week at a time.
