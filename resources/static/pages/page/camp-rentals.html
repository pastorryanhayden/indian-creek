Spring, Fall, and Winter Rental:

  1. Any overnight stay is considered a day regardless of arrival time. 

  2. There is no charge for the final departure day, if departing before 11:00 am. 

Meals: 

You are welcome to prepare your own meals and use our kitchen contingent on the following: 

  1. You have an experienced cook. 

  2. You are responsible for any missing kitchen items. 

  3. You meet with our cook to be briefed on kitchen rules, use, and safety. 

  4. If you choose for us to provide the meals, we will have a set menu you may preview upon request. 

*We are open to a few possible changes, if discussed far enough in advance to make the changes. 

Summer Rentals: 

  1. All summer weeks are taken except for a few weeks in August. This would be perfect for back to school retreats! 

  2. Summer is our Prime Time and rates tend to be higher than our spring, fall, and winter retreat pricing. 

*The summer pricing is also determined on different needs such as needing our lifeguards and swimming facilities.
