🌲 LEGACY CAMP

Making Memories. Building a Legacy.

Men and boys, join us August 14–15 at Indian Creek Baptist Camp for Legacy Camp—a weekend created to strengthen your faith, deepen relationships, and intentionally invest in the next generation.

Whether you're bringing your son, grandson, or a friend, Legacy Camp is about more than just having fun—it's about personal growth and pointing the next generation to Christ.

Your registration includes:

🎣 Fishing Tournament

🏊 Swimming

🌽 Cornhole Competition

🥏 Frisbee Golf

🎯 Air Rifle Competition

⛳ Mini Golf

🔥 Campfire & Fellowship

📖 Christ-centered Preaching

🤝 Meaningful Fellowship

🍔 Meals & Lodging

Legacy Camp is more than an outdoor getaway—it's an opportunity to create memories that last a lifetime while leaving a spiritual legacy that lasts for eternity.

"We will not hide them from their children... That the generation to come might know them... That they might set their hope in God." — Psalm 78:4, 6–7

Important Information:

• Open to men and boys (boys must be entering 1st grade or above)

• Young men under 18 must attend with an adult chaperone

• 📅 August 14–15 (check-in begins at 4pm, dinner begins at 6pm)

• 📍 Indian Creek Baptist Camp

• 💲 $30 per person
